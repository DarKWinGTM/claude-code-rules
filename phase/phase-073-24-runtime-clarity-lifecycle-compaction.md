## Selected Semantics

- Necessary apply/build/restart/reload handoff names exact action, owner, why pending and consequence. Continue authorized safe work or directly request the enabling user action; source changes do not invent activation/live-proof requirements.
- Complex explanations contain a plain synthesis of the central pain point and causal meaning. Details may come first; no mandatory opening, three-part formula or invented cause.
- Completed, remaining/untested, blocked and unselected proposals are visibly distinct when mixed. Blockers name owner/enabling condition; proposals are not obligations.
- Review selected obligations at start/resume, corrections/failures/scope changes and material status/closeout. Preserve unaffected proof and use existing records, not tasks/checklists/reruns per command.
- Reusable source/tool/test logic stays canonical. Run-bound approval/resume evidence needs private retained owner-controlled storage throughout its required lifetime; recreatable disposable scratch may be temporary. Temp/sandbox/cache/container/tool workspace and project `.tmp/` names do not guarantee durability. Missing approval-bound evidence does not authorize a replacement baseline.
- Preserve owner/trigger/action/force/exception/proof/approval/recovery semantics across compaction. Nineteen membership and installers remain unchanged; reduction is not a quota.
