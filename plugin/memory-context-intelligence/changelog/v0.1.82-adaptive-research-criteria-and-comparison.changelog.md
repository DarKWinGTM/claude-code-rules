## What Changed

The existing manual `/memory-context-intelligence:summary` contract now treats initial criteria and candidate approaches as provisional. Public research may reveal missing, misplaced or no-longer-useful criteria and approaches absent from the initial local analysis. When the decision frame changes, the workflow requires re-comparing all still-viable approaches under the revised criteria instead of preserving a stale ranking.

The expected reasoning traces initial analysis → material research findings → criteria/option/principle changes or justified no-change, with a reason. This is an adaptive decision-evidence spine, not a fixed answer taxonomy, count, report template, score or forced winner. Existing full-topic coverage, provenance, privacy, no-write/no-corpus-rerun/no-promotion and one-topic-per-artifact boundaries remain.
