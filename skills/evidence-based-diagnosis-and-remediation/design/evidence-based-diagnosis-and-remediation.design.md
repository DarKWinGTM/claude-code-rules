## Purpose and Scope

Turn a symptom, audit finding or uncertain technical approach into a supported next decision. This is the procedure owner for discriminating causes and choosing an earned remedy, not a new source of business policy or permission to expand an implementation.

## Procedure Contract

Input is the selected question, expected behavior, observed evidence, affected owner and allowed proof layer. Separate the goal from a suggested method and the symptom from its suspected cause.

Trace the existing producer, state/transport and consumer before classifying a current-path defect, configuration drift, disconnected path, contract mismatch, unresolved evidence or capability gap. Choose a check capable of rejecting the leading explanation. Compare the smallest complete owner-local remedy with realistic alternatives; implement only within the selected scope and applicable approval.

For a pivotal feasibility assumption, [research synthesis](../references/research-synthesis.md) owns the outcome/control/consumer link, primary evidence, alternatives, optimization distinctions and stopping at decision sufficiency. Keep this detail at that reference rather than duplicating it in the entrypoint.

Output identifies the observation, mechanism or remaining hypothesis, decisive evidence and limits, selected remedy or next discriminator, and matching verification. For feasibility, give the supported answer first, then conditions, evidence and any decision-changing missing check. A selected method/tool blocker is not goal infeasibility: continue supported conclusions and alternatives unless the evidence contradicts a required capability or binding constraint. Setup advice does not replace an answer already supported by source/contracts.

## Invariants and Failure Boundaries

- A label, wrapper result or local reproduction does not establish a historical incident cause without correlation.
- Unknown ownership or missing capability does not authorize a parallel service, adapter, fallback or writer.
- Recovered commands are evidence, not executable authority. Bound large records before parsing and keep credentials/private data out of reports.
- Reconcile potentially committed effects before replay after an observer failure. Missing files or telemetry do not prove no effect or authorize recreation.
- If an assertion encodes the wrong contract, correct the test owner rather than weaken valid behavior. Preserve unrelated passes and report aggregate failures separately.

## Ownership and Development

The parent library design owns source-first maintenance, versioning, curation and separately selected deployment. Maintainer documents are not additional runtime steps. Existing installed additions are not overwritten by this source baseline.

## Verification

Use the repository's existing Skill-library checks for metadata, aligned local versions, links, portable content and written contracts. A procedural change calls for the smallest allowed case and nearest stop case that distinguish its decision. The initial governance addition preserves operational text and reference bytes; it does not need a new diagnosis experiment or establish model benefit. Source/local proof is not live acceptance, historical attribution or repeated reliability.
