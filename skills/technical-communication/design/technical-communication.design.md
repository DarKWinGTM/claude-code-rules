## Purpose and Scope

Explain checked technical meaning, findings, results and next actions so a reader can understand the consequence or make the requested decision. Clear wording does not create business semantics, proof, permission or completion.

## Procedure Contract

Input is the reader's actual question, audience, working language, requested depth and checked evidence. Distinguish a definition, comparison, diagnosis, command, status or decision before choosing the answer's shape.

Resolve unfamiliar identifiers through their producer, consumer, units and contract. Lead with meaning, then the needed mechanism and consequence. Put material limits, dependencies and actions beside the relevant conclusion; use a table or ordered steps only when the relationship warrants it.

Complete the requested inventory or coherent command without inventing inputs. Retract unsupported advice explicitly while preserving valid goals and settled constraints. Finish at the useful next decision rather than a transcript, repeated recap or new unselected work.

Output is a proportionate explanation with identifiable facts, inference, uncertainty, actual actions/checks and material remainder. External copy minimizes unnecessary sensitive detail without withholding relevant evidence from the authorized owner.

## Invariants and Failure Boundaries

- Inspected, edited, configured, queued, tested, installed and published are different states; use the one supported by actual evidence.
- A success status, build or local/fake check proves its own layer, not full operation, live adoption or stability.
- Keep command formats and dependent steps coherent. Missing inputs make an example a template, not a ready-to-run recipe; preserve failure propagation and consequential warnings.
- A complete inventory retains every selected item and disposition before prioritization. Acknowledgment checks do not require a new plan.
- Explaining a proposal or next action does not authorize executing it.

## Ownership and Development

This Skill owns human-facing explanation procedure, not diagnosis, acceptance thresholds, scope selection or another policy layer. Local design/history and source-first maintenance follow the parent library contract. Existing runtime wording is independently reconciled before any selected deployment.

## Verification

Use existing Skill-library metadata/link/portability and written-contract checks. For a changed instruction, inspect a representative answer and the nearest case where confident wording or an implied action would exceed the evidence. The initial governance addition changes navigation only and preserves the operational body. Source and synthetic checks do not prove communication benefit or live behavior.
